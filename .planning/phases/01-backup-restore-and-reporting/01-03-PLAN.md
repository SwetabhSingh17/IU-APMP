### Target Multi-Sheet Excel Specification
Single `.xlsx` workbook containing:
1. **Sheet 1: Overview & Summary**
   - High-level KPIs: Total Students, Total Faculty Supervisors, Total Formed Teams, Total Topics Submitted, Total Approved Topics, Total Assigned Projects, BCA vs MCA breakdown, Evaluation Completion Rate.
2. **Sheet 2: Student Details**
   - Columns: SNo., TeamID, Enrollment, Student Name, Mobile No., Email Id, Project, Allotment. Grouped in natural Team ID alphanumeric order (e.g., A-01, A-02) with vertical cell merging across team members for TeamID, Project, and Allotment.
3. **Sheet 3: Faculty Supervisors**
   - Columns: S.No, Emp ID, Full Name (with Prefix), Designation, Department, Email, Mobile, Topics Proposed, Topics Approved, Mentored Teams, Current Mentorship Load (e.g., 3/5).
4. **Sheet 4: Project Teams (Groups)**
   - Columns: S.No, Team ID, Team Name, Course, Team Size, Leader Name, Leader Enrollment, Member Roster, Assigned Supervisor, Assigned Topic Code, Assigned Topic Title.
5. **Sheet 5: Project Topics Catalog**
   - Columns: S.No, Topic Code (PUGID), Title, Description, Course, Project Type, Technology Stack, Complexity, Proposing Supervisor, Status, Allotted Team.
6. **Sheet 6: Student Projects & Progress**
   - Columns: S.No, Project ID, Student Name, Enrollment No, Course, Topic Code, Topic Title, Supervisor, Overall Progress %, Status, Allocated Date.
7. **Sheet 7: Evaluations & Assessments**
   - Columns: S.No, Student Name, Enrollment No, Course, Project Topic, Evaluator Supervisor, Score / Marks Obtained, Feedback Comments, Assessment Date.
8. **Sheet 8: Milestones & Deadlines**
   - Columns: S.No, Project Title, Student/Team, Milestone Title, Description, Due Date, Status, Completed Date.
